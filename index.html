Our Story

Hello, Skincare Lovers!

Butter Me Up started in a small kitchen with one simple craving: a body butter fluffy enough to feel like a cloud, and honest enough to trust on your skin.

Every jar is hand-whipped in small batches using raw, unrefined shea butter — no fillers, no shortcuts, just ingredients you can actually pronounce. From nut to jar, we keep it simple, sustainable, and made with love.
